How the formula works

For a known percentile, the central view uses 1 + (100 − percentile) ÷ 100 × (candidate count − 1), rounded to the nearest whole position. It is a transparent estimate, not an NTA-issued AIR; ties and NTA’s final ranking process can change the official value.

Keep the data trustworthy

Use official sources for inputs that affect a real decision.

  • Copy percentile or AIR from your final NTA scorecard.
  • Use the NTA result notice for the actual unique candidate count.
  • Verify official category closing ranks in JoSAA/CSAB counselling rounds.
